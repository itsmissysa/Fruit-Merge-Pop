HOW TO PLAY

🍓
1. DROP A FRUIT

Tap or drag to choose where you want to drop your fruit.

🍊
2. MERGE FRUITS

Match two identical fruits to merge them into a bigger fruit!

🍉
3. KEEP MERGING

Create bigger and bigger fruits to earn more points and coins.

⚠️
4. DON'T OVERFLOW

Be careful! Don't let the fruits pile up beyond the danger line.

🪙
5. EARN COINS

Use your coins to unlock cute fruit styles in the Shop.

💡

TIP!
Plan your drops carefully and create chain merges for a higher score!
